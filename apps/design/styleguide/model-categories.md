recognition goes by the folder's contents: the names of the subfolders are the
categories `NFR-390`. Anything unrecognised is shown separately rather than
passed over in silence. Custom nodes get a line of their own: the mechanism
would work on that folder too, but sharing it would undo the very reason for
keeping separate instances, so it is excluded unconditionally and with an
explanation `US-SHARED-02/AC-4`.
