## Can someone sell this?

Yes — and that surprises people, so it is worth being precise.

The GPL permits charging money for a copy. What it does not permit is
charging money and keeping the source. Whoever sells you a build owes you
its source under the GPL, and once you have it you may publish it for
everyone, free, immediately. That is why a paid fork does not stay paid for
long: the licence does not forbid the price, it removes the ability to
defend it.

Charging for support, setup, training or hosting is fine, and always was.
That is a service, not a toll on the program.

**If you paid someone for this app, you did not have to.** The official
build is free, here: [Download](/download).

## Forks and the name

The GPL covers the code. It does not hand over the name.

"ComfyUI Portable Organizer", the short form "CPO", and the project's
icons are not part of what the licence grants. A fork may do everything the
GPL allows — including selling itself, source and all — but it ships under
its own name, so that you can tell whose build you are running. Saying
where it came from is fine and always will be: *"a fork of ComfyUI Portable
Organizer"* is an accurate sentence, not a trademark problem.
